The Reward Deals Playbook

How I’m using reward deals to fund my Christmas shopping, and how you can plan yours.

Where I am right now: I’ve completed Level 2 of the program (2 deals). I’m sharing this as I go, so everything below is what I’ve learned so far plus the plan I’m following for the next levels. I’ll update it as I move up.

What this program is

The program pays gift card rewards when you complete a set number of “deals.” The more deals you complete, the bigger the reward tier you can claim. Here are the tiers exactly as shown on the program’s site when I took this screenshot.

  • Higher tiers pay more per deal, but they need more deals, more time and more chances for a deal to cost you something.
  • Pick a tier you can finish before you need the money. A small reward you claim beats a big one you abandon.
  • Set your target before you start and write it down.

Step 3: Claim your reward

  • When you reach your tier, follow the program’s own claim steps. The exact process may differ from anything I describe here.
  • Choose a gift card that works where you plan to shop. Check that your store accepts that card type before you plan around it.
  • Don’t count on the money until the card arrives. Delivery and verification can take time.

Step 4: Plan your Christmas shopping backwards

  • Write your Christmas shopping budget and the date you need the money by.
  • Count backwards: shopping days, delivery time, deal time. Start early.
  • Treat any reward as a bonus toward your list, not as money you’ve already earned.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping the terms and getting charged for a trial you forgot to cancel.
  • Spending more on deals than the reward is worth.
  • Starting too late and missing your Christmas deadline.
  • Using your main email, so confirmations get lost.
  • Aiming for the biggest tier on day one.
